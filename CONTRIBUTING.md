# Contributing

This repository is an archival scientific record. Concrete numerical results, closed-chart ranges, the Stokes residual, and the status of the Barrett–Crane $10j$ are preserved as recorded in the Final Report of 25 October 2026 and are not open for modification.

## Acceptable Contributions

- Corrections of typographical errors in documentation.
- Clarifications that do not alter scientific claims.
- Additions of bibliographic references that improve the literature comparison.
- Improvements to formatting or citation metadata that enhance citability.

## Not Accepted

- Changes to numerical values, residual figures, or closed-chart descriptions.
- Proposals to reopen closed charts without an explicit new on-shell Barrett seed and EqGate admission (see the Superseding Policy in the README).
- Identification of $\Sigma_{S_q}$ with $\Sigma_{\mathrm{ReS}}$.

## Process

1. Open an issue describing the proposed change.
2. If the change is accepted, submit a pull request against the `main` branch.
3. All contributions are subject to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

For questions regarding the scientific content, contact twenty7delta@gmail.com.
